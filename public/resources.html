Farm & Livestock Guides

  • Plan before you purchase. Start with the animals, land, shelter, water, feed storage, fencing and daily labor—not just the purchase price.
  • Track recurring costs. Feed, bedding, repairs, utilities and transport can matter as much as initial setup costs.
  • Build for your conditions. Soil, drainage, weather, predator pressure and local rules affect what works on a particular property.

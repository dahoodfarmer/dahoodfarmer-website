★ Advertise with Da HoodFarmer ★

Relevant brands. Useful information. Clearly identified partnerships.

Da HoodFarmer is developing an independent publishing platform around agriculture, livestock, land care, outdoor readiness and entrepreneurship. We are interested in advertising relationships that bring relevant products and services to readers without compromising editorial trust.

Available partnership formats

  • Sponsored resource: A clearly labeled educational feature supported by a relevant company. Sponsorship does not guarantee a positive review or editorial control.
  • Display placement: A labeled banner or placement on an eligible page, subject to inventory, technical requirements and platform policies.
  • Newsletter sponsorship: A labeled sponsor placement in a future email edition, subject to audience size and delivery performance.
  • Category sponsorship: A clearly marked sponsor association with a relevant resource category or guide series.
  • Qualified referral partnership: A disclosed arrangement for legitimate customer inquiries where a provider pays for an agreed, valid referral.

Who may be a fit?

Potential categories include farm and ranch suppliers, feed and livestock equipment providers, fencing and land-care services, outdoor equipment, workwear, agricultural education and South Carolina businesses whose services are relevant to readers.

Audience and pricing transparency

We do not publish invented traffic totals, follower counts, conversion rates or advertising prices. Placement availability and pricing will be based on verified analytics, audience geography, page performance, format and campaign scope. Request a media information package for current verified figures when available.

Editorial standards

  • Paid placements will be labeled as advertising or sponsored content.
  • Affiliate relationships will be disclosed near relevant links.
  • Advertisers cannot buy an undisclosed endorsement or guaranteed editorial conclusion.
  • We will not sell fake traffic, incentivized ad clicks, misleading placements or bot-generated engagement.
  • All partnerships are subject to written terms and applicable platform policies.

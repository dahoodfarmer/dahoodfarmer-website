Work with Da HoodFarmer

Da HoodFarmer is building an independent media and resource platform rooted in South Carolina, with content spanning agriculture, livestock, land stewardship, outdoor skills, entrepreneurship, and media. We welcome inquiries from businesses whose products and services genuinely serve that audience.

Available partnership formats

  • Display sponsorship: Clearly labeled sponsor placements on selected public content pages, subject to availability and technical suitability.
  • Sponsored resource guide: A useful, clearly identified guide or educational feature supported by a relevant business. Sponsor influence and editorial boundaries are agreed in writing.
  • Newsletter sponsorship: A labeled sponsor message in a future newsletter edition, subject to audience size and send schedule.
  • Video and social integrations: Brand mentions or product demonstrations when the subject is relevant and the relationship is disclosed.
  • Referral partnerships: Qualified introductions for relevant services under a written agreement. No lead volume or conversion is guaranteed.

Audience and performance transparency

We do not publish invented traffic figures, audience demographics, impressions, or conversion guarantees. Current reach and available inventory will be shared from verified analytics when preparing a proposal. Pricing will be based on actual audience data, placement, production effort, duration, and deliverables—not inflated estimates.

Editorial standards

  • Paid placements are labeled as advertising or sponsored content.
  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed near relevant recommendations.
  • We do not sell fake reviews, guaranteed outcomes, or undisclosed endorsements.
  • We reserve the right to decline products or services that do not fit the audience or cannot be substantiated.
  • Any campaign terms, payment schedule, usage rights, and deliverables are agreed in writing before publication.

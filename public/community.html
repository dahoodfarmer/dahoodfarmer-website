The Mission

Community is at the heart of everything Da HoodFarmer does. From agricultural education to wellness awareness to mentorship, the goal is to build up the next generation while staying rooted in the values that built the brand.

Agricultural Literacy

Teaching people where their food comes from, how farms work, and why agriculture matters. Through farm visits, workshops, and educational content, Da HoodFarmer connects communities to the land.

Wellness & Healing Ain't Soft

The Healing Ain't Soft movement extends into community through real conversations about men's health, mental wellness, and self-care. Breaking stigmas, building confidence.

Youth & Mentorship

Investing in young people through mentorship, outdoor education, and agricultural programs. Sharing the skills and values that come from farming, military-school leadership experience, and wilderness survival instruction. Da HoodFarmer is also a two-time PWC Beat the Odds honoree.

About the Creator

Da HoodFarmer is a South Carolina-based farmer and agricultural entrepreneur whose work connects practical agriculture with entertainment, rural lifestyle, mechanical know-how and personal storytelling. The brand is grounded in hands-on experience rather than a passive-investor identity.

Background includes Certified Master Farmer credentials, experience working with an 88-acre farm, and practical familiarity with livestock and small-farm operations—including rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs and horses. Additional experience includes military-school leadership and wilderness-survival instruction.

On-screen credit: Lifetime’s Dr. Pimple Popper, “Holy Moly” (Season 1, Episode 7).

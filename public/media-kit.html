About the Creator

Loopy Carter is the public personality behind Da HoodFarmer, an agricultural, cowboy-lifestyle and media brand. The work connects practical agriculture with entertainment, rural lifestyle, mechanical know-how and personal storytelling, grounded in hands-on experience rather than a passive-investor identity.

Background includes Certified Master Farmer credentials and hands-on experience across an 88-acre farm; that figure describes experience, not current ownership. Practical livestock and small-farm experience includes rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs and horses. Additional experience includes leadership at Massanutten Military Academy (military-school experience, not armed-forces service) and wilderness-survival instruction.

On-screen credit: Lifetime’s Dr. Pimple Popper, “Holy Moly” (Season 1, Episode 7).

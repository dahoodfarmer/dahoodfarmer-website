DHF Opportunity Network

DHF Academy

Practical knowledge. Real-world experience. Better opportunities.

DHF Academy is the educational and workshop division of the Opportunity Network. Programs are designed around practical skills, entrepreneurship, agriculture, land stewardship and the modern creator economy.

Farm Foundations

Small-farm planning, specialty crops, livestock fundamentals and operational readiness.

Land & Stewardship

Land-use education, property research, agritourism planning and responsible land management.

Business Building

Business models, marketing, customer development, operations and partnership readiness.

Contracting Readiness

Understanding procurement pathways, capability statements and responsible preparation.

Media & Creator Skills

Content development, brand positioning, storytelling and digital audience growth.

How programs may be delivered

Virtual workshops, in-person classes, demonstrations, group training and organization-specific sessions. Program format, curriculum, instructors, dates and fees will be stated before registration opens.

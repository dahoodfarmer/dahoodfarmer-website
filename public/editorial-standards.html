Editorial Standards & Corrections

Useful information. Clear sourcing. Honest commercial relationships.

Da HoodFarmer publishes educational resources about agriculture, livestock, land care, outdoor readiness and entrepreneurship. These standards explain how information is prepared, how commercial relationships are disclosed and how readers can flag an error.

Research and sourcing

  • We prioritize primary sources such as government agencies, university extension programs, product manuals and official program pages.
  • We link to important sources so readers can check the original information.
  • We distinguish general examples and estimates from verified local prices, professional specifications or guarantees.
  • Where rules, prices, program eligibility or deadlines can change, we direct readers to the responsible organization for current information.
  • We do not invent statistics, quotes, endorsements, product testing or personal experiences.

AI-assisted preparation

Some research, outlining and drafting may be assisted by AI tools. AI-assisted material is checked against cited sources before publication where practical. AI is not a substitute for field testing, professional judgment, veterinary care, legal advice or a qualified inspection. We do not present generated estimates as measured results or claim first-hand testing that did not occur.

Corrections and updates

If you find an error, outdated link or unclear statement, contact us through the contact page and include the page URL, the statement in question and a reliable source where possible. We will review valid reports and update material when warranted. Significant corrections should be reflected in the page's updated date.

Advertising and affiliate relationships

  • Advertising and sponsored content will be labeled clearly.
  • Affiliate links will be disclosed close to the link or recommendation.
  • Payment does not buy a favorable editorial conclusion or undisclosed endorsement.
  • We do not sell fake traffic, bot engagement, incentivized ad clicks or misleading placements.
  • Commercial partners do not control independent editorial conclusions.

Read our affiliate disclosure and advertising and sponsorship information.

Limitations

Educational resources are general information. Conditions vary by location, species, equipment, land and individual circumstances. Confirm critical decisions with the relevant agency, manufacturer, extension service or qualified professional.

From South Carolina Soil to the Screen

Based in Columbia, South Carolina, Da HoodFarmer is a Certified Master Farmer, cowboy, agricultural entrepreneur, and television personality. His farm experience includes working across an 88-acre operation with rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs, and horses.

His background also includes Recreation & Leisure Studies at Benedict College, military experience and leadership at Massanutten Military Academy, and service as a Senior Wilderness Survival Instructor.

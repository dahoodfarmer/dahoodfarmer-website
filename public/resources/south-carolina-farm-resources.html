South Carolina Farm Resources

Official starting points for education, land access and conservation assistance.

South Carolina farmers and landowners can use university extension, state agricultural programs and federal conservation offices to find practical information and determine which services may fit their operation. This page organizes official starting points; it does not promise funding, program acceptance or a particular result.

1. Clemson Cooperative Extension

Clemson Cooperative Extension is a state resource for research-based education and local extension support. Use it to find information and assistance relevant to agriculture, animals, horticulture, food systems and natural resources. For animal care or production questions, look for the specific subject-matter program and current guidance.

2. South Carolina Department of Agriculture

The SC Department of Agriculture Farm & Business Support page brings together state resources covering agribusiness development, agricultural organizations, emergency planning, fruit and vegetable farming, grants, marketing support, the SC Agricultural Tax Exemption program and SC Farm Link.

3. SC Farm Link: land access and transition

SC Farm Link is a statewide farmland transition program administered by the South Carolina Department of Agriculture. The program connects beginning or expanding farmers looking for land with landowners interested in keeping property in agricultural use. The agency lists farmlink@scda.sc.gov and 803-734-2210 as contact details on its program page. Confirm current participation steps and available acreage directly with the program.

4. USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service in South Carolina

The USDA NRCS South Carolina office provides conservation planning and technical assistance, with financial assistance available through programs subject to eligibility, ranking and funding availability. Assistance may address soil, water, plants, animals and other natural-resource concerns. Program requirements and application deadlines can change, so contact the local service center before making plans around a funding opportunity.

Columbia state office contact shown by NRCS:
1835 Assembly Street, Suite 950, Columbia, SC 29201-2448
Phone: 803-253-3935
Verify current office details with NRCS.

5. Prepare before contacting an agency

  • Write down your operation, goals and the specific problem you need help solving.
  • Identify the county and the land you own or lease.
  • Gather a basic map, acreage information and a list of existing improvements.
  • Prepare questions about eligibility, application windows, documentation and next steps.
  • Keep copies of applications, quotes, agreements and communications.

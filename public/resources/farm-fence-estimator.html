Before purchasing fence materials

  • Identify what the fence must contain or exclude; livestock, pets and wildlife needs are different.
  • Walk the line and account for slopes, drainage, trees, property boundaries and access points.
  • Count corners, ends, braces and gates separately. These often require different posts and hardware.
  • Check local rules, easements and property lines before construction.
  • Get written quotes from suppliers and qualified installers where needed.

Important limitation

This tool is a budgeting aid only. It does not specify a safe fence design, post depth, wire gauge, energizer, bracing method or livestock containment standard. Those depend on the site, soil, animal species and fence type.

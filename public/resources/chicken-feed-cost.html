Chicken Feed Cost Calculator

Estimate your flock's feed budget using your own numbers.

Feed is a recurring expense, so it helps to estimate consumption before setting a flock budget. This calculator uses the daily feed amount you enter; it does not assume every bird, breed, age or season has the same needs.

Planning estimate only. Actual consumption and costs vary with bird age, size, breed, forage access, feed formulation, waste, weather and management. Follow the feed label and appropriate poultry guidance.

How to use the estimate

  1. Enter the number of birds you currently keep or plan to keep.
  2. Enter your expected average daily feed per bird. Use a value from your feed guidance or your own measured usage.
  3. Enter the actual bag weight and price you pay.
  4. Use the 30-day estimate as one line in a broader livestock budget, then add bedding, water, housing, health care, repairs and other costs.

What this calculator does not include

This is a feed-cost estimate, not a ration formulation or animal-health recommendation. It does not calculate protein, energy, calcium or other nutritional requirements. Those depend on the birds' age and purpose and should be checked against reliable poultry-extension guidance and the feed manufacturer's label.

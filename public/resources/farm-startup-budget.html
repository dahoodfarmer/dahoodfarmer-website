Small Farm Startup Budget Calculator

Add your own estimates to organize a first-pass setup budget.

Before buying equipment, livestock or materials, organize the main setup costs. This calculator adds the amounts you enter; it does not estimate supplier prices, operating income, loan payments or whether a farm will be profitable.

Enter actual local quotes where possible. This total excludes recurring feed, fuel, utilities, labor, insurance, taxes, rent, repairs and debt payments. Build a separate operating budget and cash-flow plan before committing funds.

Use this total carefully

  • Record the source and date for each estimate.
  • Include delivery, taxes, installation and required accessories in the relevant category.
  • Separate one-time setup costs from recurring operating expenses.
  • Check local rules, permits, property boundaries and insurance requirements.
  • Keep a reserve that reflects the risks and timing of your specific operation.

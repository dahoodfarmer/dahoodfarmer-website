What these workshops cover

Clemson describes the workshops as practical introductions to the realities of starting a farm business. Topics include business, land and financial considerations, connecting with agricultural and business development resources, and identifying next steps. In-person sessions include a farm tour and opportunities to meet resource providers.

Before registering

  • Confirm the event is still accepting registrations and check the current fee.
  • Review the exact location, schedule, lunch arrangements and transportation needs.
  • Prepare questions about land access, startup costs, business planning and local technical assistance.
  • Do not assume a workshop provides a grant, loan, land placement or business approval.

Official media resources for Loopy Carter & Da HoodFarmer

Short Biography

Loopy Carter is a Columbia, South Carolina-based public personality, Certified Master Farmer, cowboy, agricultural entrepreneur, and television personality, also known through the Da HoodFarmer brand. His background includes hands-on experience across an 88-acre farm, Recreation & Leisure Studies at Benedict College, military-school leadership at Massanutten Military Academy, and work as a Senior Wilderness Survival Instructor. His Beat the Odds recognition includes the Phoenix Award, a college scholarship, and a Distinguished Speaker appearance. He appeared on Lifetime's Dr. Pimple Popper.

Extended Biography

Rooted in DMV family history and based in South Carolina, Loopy Carter and the Da HoodFarmer brand bring together agriculture, livestock, land stewardship, entrepreneurship, outdoor education, and media. His farm experience includes rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs, and horses. At Massanutten Military Academy in Woodstock, Virginia, he earned the rank of Second Lieutenant and received the Commandant's Accommodations Medal for helping save two cadets. As a Senior Wilderness Survival Instructor, he shares practical outdoor and preparedness skills through workshops and remote instruction. The Healing Ain't Soft movement extends the brand into men's skincare, confidence, wellness awareness, and honest conversations about self-care.

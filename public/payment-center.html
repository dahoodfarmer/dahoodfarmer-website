How an approved sponsor payment will work

  1. DHF and the sponsor agree to written partnership terms.
  2. The owner records the signed agreement and approved amount in the private Sponsor CRM.
  3. DHF issues a unique invoice and secure payment link. A matching QR code may be provided for convenience.
  4. The sponsor chooses an available payment method on the authorized provider's secure checkout.
  5. DHF records payment only after verified provider confirmation or owner-verified bank settlement.

Questions about an existing agreement or invoice? Use the official contact page. Never pay an invoice that you cannot independently verify with Da HoodFarmer.

DHF Opportunity Network

Sponsorships & Partnerships

Put your brand behind experiences that bring people and opportunity together.

DHF works with companies, institutions and organizations that want to support agriculture, entrepreneurship, education, media and community experiences. Sponsorship opportunities are matched to the specific event, audience, geography and deliverables.

Ways to partner

Presenting Partner

Top-level association with a flagship event or program, subject to an agreed sponsorship package.

Supporting Sponsor

Support a program, workshop, educational stage or community experience.

Exhibitor Partner

Demonstrate products and services through an approved event or marketplace activation.

Media Partner

Explore clearly disclosed content, interview, production and promotional collaborations.

In-Kind Partner

Discuss equipment, services, supplies or venue support with defined deliverables.

What a proposal can include

  • Event or program association.
  • Approved logo placement and sponsor recognition.
  • Exhibitor or demonstration opportunities.
  • Speaking or educational participation where appropriate.
  • Co-created content and media placements, clearly disclosed as sponsored.
  • Post-event delivery summary for agreed sponsor benefits.

We do not publish unverified audience figures or guarantee sales, impressions or outcomes. Each sponsorship is documented with its scope, fee, timing, placement and delivery requirements before payment is requested.

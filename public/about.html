South Carolina Roots

Da HoodFarmer is based in Columbia, South Carolina. The brand brings together agriculture, livestock, land stewardship, entrepreneurship, and a commitment to sharing practical knowledge. As a Certified Master Farmer, Da HoodFarmer represents an agricultural identity grounded in hands-on experience and respect for the land.

Agriculture & Livestock

Farm experience includes work across an 88-acre operation and hands-on care for rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs, and horses. That experience informs an approach to animal care, agricultural education, land stewardship, and farm-based entrepreneurship.

Education & Military Experience

Da HoodFarmer studied Recreation & Leisure Studies at Benedict College. His military-school experience includes Massanutten Military Academy in Woodstock, Virginia, where he earned the rank of Second Lieutenant and received the Commandant's Accommodations Medal for helping save two cadets. These experiences shaped a focus on discipline, leadership, responsibility, and service.

Wilderness & Survival Instruction

As a Senior Wilderness Survival Instructor, Da HoodFarmer teaches practical outdoor skills, preparedness, navigation, and survival fundamentals. Instruction can be delivered through in-person workshops and remote learning.

Awards & Recognition

Da HoodFarmer is a two-time PWC Beat the Odds honoree. This recognition is part of a broader story of persistence, personal growth, and turning challenges into forward momentum.

Television Appearance

Da HoodFarmer appeared on Lifetime's Dr. Pimple Popper, "Holy Moly," Season 1, Episode 7. The appearance introduced the brand to a national television audience and created opportunities for further media and speaking engagements.

The Healing Ain't Soft Movement

Beyond farming and media, Da HoodFarmer leads the Healing Ain't Soft initiative — a men's skincare, confidence, and wellness-awareness movement that encourages honest conversations about self-care.

South Carolina Roots

Da HoodFarmer is rooted in South Carolina, where agriculture, livestock, and a connection to the land shape a way of life. From the soil up, the brand represents an authentic agricultural identity — one built on hard work, respect for the land, and a commitment to community.

Education & Military Service

Before the farm and the camera, there was education and military service. These experiences instilled discipline, resilience, and a sense of duty that carry through every venture today.

Agriculture & Livestock

Working with livestock and managing agricultural land is more than a livelihood — it's a calling. Da HoodFarmer brings hands-on experience in farming, animal husbandry, and land stewardship to every project and partnership.

Wilderness & Survival Instruction

With a background in outdoor and wilderness survival instruction, Da HoodFarmer teaches others how to navigate, endure, and thrive in the natural world. This expertise is available for workshops, events, and educational programs.

Television Appearance

Da HoodFarmer appeared on Lifetime's Dr. Pimple Popper, "Holy Moly," Season 1, Episode 7. The appearance introduced the brand to a national television audience and opened doors for media opportunities.

The Healing Ain't Soft Movement

Beyond farming and media, Da HoodFarmer leads the Healing Ain't Soft initiative — a men's skincare, confidence, and wellness-awareness movement that encourages real conversations about self-care.

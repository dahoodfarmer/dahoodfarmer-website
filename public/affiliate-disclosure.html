How commercial relationships are identified.

Display advertising

This website is configured to load the Google AdSense library using its publisher identifier. Loading the library does not mean ads are approved or currently being served. Ad serving depends on Google's review, account standing, settings and applicable policies. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize and measure ads, subject to applicable consent requirements and user controls.

Affiliate links

No third-party affiliate product links are currently activated in the Resource Center. If a reader later purchases through a qualifying affiliate link, Da HoodFarmer may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Each affiliate link will be identified close to the recommendation or link, not only on this page. Recommendations must remain relevant to the subject and disclose material relationships.

For Amazon links, the required statement will be used where applicable: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”

Sponsored content and paid placements

Paid articles, newsletter placements, display placements and other sponsorships will be clearly labeled with terms such as “Advertisement” or “Sponsored.” A sponsor does not purchase an undisclosed editorial endorsement. Editorial conclusions will not be represented as independent when they are paid for.

Paid referrals

If a business pays for a qualified referral or lead, the relationship will be disclosed in the relevant context. Referral terms and what qualifies as a valid lead will be agreed in writing.

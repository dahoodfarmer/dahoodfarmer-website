Tools for people doing the work

This resource center is being built around practical farm operations, livestock, land care, equipment planning, and rural living. Start with the free calculators below. Enter your own prices and quantities; the estimates are based on your inputs, not hidden assumptions.

How to use these estimates: These tools are planning aids, not supplier quotes or veterinary, engineering, or financial advice. Actual material needs, animal feed requirements, local rules, taxes, delivery costs, waste, terrain, and installation requirements vary. Verify purchases and plans with the relevant supplier or qualified professional.

Before sending materials

Do not submit confidential show or franchise material through the general contact form.

Do not send unreleased concepts, formats, scripts, episode arcs, character biographies, AI character bibles, budgets, casting plans, sizzle reels, production mechanics, or other proprietary materials in an unsolicited inquiry.

Initial conversations should remain high-level and non-confidential. Before a formal submission, we review the recipient's submission terms, rights, exclusivity, options, ownership, permitted use and reversion provisions. An NDA is not assumed to be available.

How to start a conversation

Use the contact form for a non-confidential introduction. Identify your organization, role, production or distribution capabilities, relevant experience and the type of relationship you want to discuss. Do not attach confidential pitch materials.

Da HoodFarmer Opportunity Network

Business Network

Build relationships. Find opportunities. Put your business in the room.

The DHF Business Network connects entrepreneurs, agricultural producers, creators, service providers, sponsors and organizations with events, educational programs, business visibility and property-based experiences.

Our reach is national: all 50 U.S. states are welcome, and international inquiries are considered where the opportunity, regulations and logistics make sense.

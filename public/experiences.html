DHF Opportunity Network

Land & Experiences

Turn the right property into a place for learning, connection and commerce.

A property does not have to be owned by DHF to become part of a well-designed experience. We explore partnerships with farms, ranches, landowners, rural venues, tourism operators and organizations that have a suitable place and a clear commercial opportunity.

Agritourism

Farm visits, seasonal experiences, crop demonstrations and agricultural learning.

Markets & Festivals

Outdoor vendor markets, agricultural showcases and approved community events.

Retreats & Gatherings

Private group experiences, business retreats and team-building concepts.

Outdoor Education

Practical outdoor skills, land stewardship and supervised educational activities.

Media & Production

Photography, filming and content-production location partnerships.

How property partnerships work

We assess the property, proposed use, audience, commercial terms and operating responsibilities. A site is not promoted or booked until the owner has authorized its use and the necessary written agreements, permits, insurance, safety arrangements and land-use approvals are confirmed.

Property partner inquiry

Introduce your property or venue

Tell us where the property is, what it can support and what kind of partnership you have in mind. Do not submit access codes, private security details or sensitive property documents through this form.

An inquiry does not create a lease, booking, agency relationship or commitment to use the property.

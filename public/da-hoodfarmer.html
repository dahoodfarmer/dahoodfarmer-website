Da HoodFarmer is the agricultural, cowboy-lifestyle, and media brand founded and represented by Loopy Carter. A brand built from lived experience

Da HoodFarmer is not a fictional character or a costume. It is a public-facing expression of a life shaped by farming, animals, rural skills, outdoor education, leadership, and a willingness to build new opportunities. The brand is rooted in South Carolina while carrying the family history and DMV roots that shaped Loopy Carter.

Agriculture and farm life

Loopy Carter is a Certified Master Farmer with experience across an 88-acre farm. His animal and livestock experience includes rabbits, chickens, ducks, pigs, horses, cattle, geese, quail, and peafowl. The work behind the brand includes daily animal care, observation, feeding, handling, farm operations, soil and land stewardship, and practical agricultural learning.

The 88-acre reference describes experience on a farm operation; it does not imply current ownership of that property. The brand’s agricultural content is designed to share practical knowledge and encourage more people to understand farming, livestock, and the work behind food production.

Cowboy culture and outdoor knowledge

The cowboy identity is expressed through hands-on rural life, animal care, practical mechanics, and respect for the work that keeps a farm running. Loopy Carter also serves as a Senior Wilderness Survival Instructor, with experience teaching outdoor skills and survival fundamentals through both in-person and remote instruction.

Television and digital media

Da HoodFarmer is connected to Loopy Carter’s appearance on Lifetime’s Dr. Pimple Popper, in a segment known as “Holy Moly.” The brand is positioned for television, reality and documentary programming, digital video, interviews, hosting, and collaborations across agriculture, rural lifestyle, outdoor skills, tools, workwear, and related categories.

Business and projects

The brand is developing a portfolio of ideas across agricultural education, farm and livestock opportunities, media, products, digital ventures, and community initiatives.

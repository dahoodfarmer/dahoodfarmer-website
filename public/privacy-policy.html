★ Privacy Policy ★

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Da HoodFarmer operates https://dahoodfarmer.com. This policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit the site, contact us, apply to the DHF Opportunity Network, or request information about an event, workshop, sponsorship, business listing or property partnership.

Information We Collect

  • Contact and booking inquiries: Name, email, organization, inquiry type, preferred date and message.
  • Opportunity Network applications: Name, email, business or organization name, website, country, state/province/region, city, category, requested opportunity, event or program interest, preferred date, application message and the page from which the application was submitted.
  • Consent records: Whether you agreed to be contacted about an application, whether you separately opted into marketing updates, and whether you separately authorized a public business-directory profile.
  • Public directory information: If you apply for a business listing, separately authorize publication and the application is approved, we may display the approved business name, public description, website, category and business location. Private email addresses and application messages are not published in the directory.
  • Newsletter: Email address, subscription status, consent timestamp and unsubscribe token.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, request metadata and security signals used for analytics, abuse prevention and service operation.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to questions, booking requests and applications.
  • Review potential vendor, sponsor, speaker, business, education and property partnerships.
  • Plan programs and assess interest by country, region and category.
  • Publish a business profile only after approval and separate public-listing consent.
  • Send marketing or newsletter updates only when you separately opt in; you can unsubscribe.
  • Protect forms and services, prevent spam and improve the website.

Consent and Public Listings

Consent to be contacted about an application is separate from optional marketing consent. Public business-directory publication is also separate and optional. Submitting an application does not automatically publish your information, approve your business, reserve event space or create a payment obligation. You can contact us to request that a published listing be hidden or corrected.

Service Providers and Integrations

  • Cloudflare: Website hosting, serverless functions, database storage, security, bot protection and administrative access controls.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Privacy-focused aggregate usage and performance measurement. The current configuration uses its EU-exclusion mode, so EU visitors are excluded from this analytics collection; this does not restrict access to the website or application forms.
  • Google Analytics 4: Website measurement (measurement ID G-BQJHY0DYR2).
  • Google AdSense: Advertising account is pending approval; advertising may be enabled only after applicable platform and privacy requirements are met.
  • Stripe: Payment processing is not activated for DHF Opportunity Network applications. Do not enter card or bank details into website forms.
  • Email delivery: Optional transactional notifications may be configured through an email provider. An application is stored independently of optional notification delivery.

Analytics and advertising technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers. Their use can depend on your location, browser settings and applicable consent requirements. We are reviewing the consent-management requirements that apply before activating advertising for additional markets.

Storage, Transfers and Retention

Website form submissions and directory records are stored using Cloudflare services. Cloudflare operates a global network, so information may be processed in countries other than the country from which you submit it. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to review and respond to the inquiry, operate an ongoing relationship, maintain consent and publication records, prevent abuse, and meet legal or contractual obligations.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not publish private application messages or applicant email addresses in the public directory. We do not sell application information. We do not provide private applicant details to sponsors or other applicants merely because they participate in the network. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website or disclosed when required by law.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal information, withdraw optional consent, object to certain processing, or request that a public listing be hidden. You may unsubscribe from newsletter emails using the link in the email. To make a privacy request, use the contact page and identify the request you are making.

International Inquiries

We welcome inquiries from all 50 U.S. states and from other countries. Whether a particular event, product, service or partnership can be provided depends on applicable laws, privacy requirements, taxes, payment availability, permits, insurance, shipping and operational logistics.

Children

This website and its commercial application forms are not directed to children under 13. Do not submit personal information about a child unless you are authorized to do so and it is necessary for a specific, approved program.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us.

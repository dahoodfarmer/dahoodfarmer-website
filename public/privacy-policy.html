★ Privacy Policy ★

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Da HoodFarmer ("we","us","our") operates https://dahoodfarmer.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form: Name, email, inquiry category, message
  • Booking inquiries: Name, email, organization, inquiry type, preferred date, details
  • Newsletter: Email address
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited

Active Integrations

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — Spam and bot protection on all forms
  • Cloudflare Access — Admin dashboard authentication
  • Google Analytics 4 — Visitor analytics (anonymized, measurement ID G-BQJHY0DYR2)
  • Google AdSense — Display advertising (pending Google approval, publisher ID ca-pub-5452000990146137)
  • Stripe — Payment processing (not yet configured — will be activated when products are available)

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and booking requests
  • To send newsletter emails (only if subscribed)
  • To analyze traffic and improve content
  • To prevent spam via Cloudflare Turnstile

Data Storage

Form submissions and newsletter subscription records are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. For new newsletter subscriptions, we record the consent timestamp alongside the email address and subscription status. Unsubscribe requests are processed using a unique link token. Data is processed on Cloudflare's global network.

Your Rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from newsletter at any time

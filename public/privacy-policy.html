Advertising, analytics and cookies

Google Analytics 4 is configured with Google Consent Mode v2 defaults set to denied for analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization. If consent remains denied, Google tags may send limited cookieless measurement signals but should not read or write cookies for those denied categories. A compatible consent management platform must update the consent signals after the visitor's choice. The Google AdSense library is disabled by default and will only load after Google approval and explicit owner activation following consent-platform setup. No personalized advertising should be enabled before the required Google-certified consent management platform is configured for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Google Analytics may process page and interaction information according to the consent state and Google's data practices.

You can review or change Google's personalized advertising settings at Google Ads Settings and learn about Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Before enabling personalized advertising for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site must use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the applicable consent framework where required. This privacy notice does not itself collect or record consent; the configured consent management platform must provide and persist the applicable consent signals.

THE STORY

Agriculture is the foundation.
Opportunity is the mission.

Da HoodFarmer is an agriculture-centered personal brand exploring the connections between land, practical knowledge, media and business. The goal is to make agriculture visible, accessible and connected to new opportunities.

Based in Columbia, South Carolina, this platform brings the projects, stories and partnerships together in one place.

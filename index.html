From South Carolina Soil to the Screen

Rooted in South Carolina, Da HoodFarmer built a life around agriculture, livestock, and the land. With a background in education and military service, the journey includes outdoor and wilderness survival instruction, entrepreneurial ventures, and a television appearance.

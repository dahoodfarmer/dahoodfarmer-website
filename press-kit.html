Short Biography

Da HoodFarmer is a South Carolina-based farmer, cowboy, entrepreneur, and television personality. Rooted in agriculture and livestock, the brand spans farming, wilderness survival instruction, the Healing Ain't Soft wellness movement, and community advocacy. Da HoodFarmer appeared on Lifetime's Dr. Pimple Popper, "Holy Moly," Season 1, Episode 7.

Brand Summary

Da HoodFarmer represents authentic agricultural identity, entrepreneurial drive, and community commitment. The brand's pillars are agriculture, television and media, the Healing Ain't Soft movement, business ventures, and community engagement.

# DHF Media, Network and Distribution Prospect Map

**Internal planning document — 17 November 2026**
**Status:** Research only. No submissions, pitches, calls or messages have been sent.
**Purpose:** Separate sponsors from production vendors, development companies, broadcasters, distribution platforms and public-sector support resources.

## Executive distinction

The media organizations in this map are not interchangeable, and most are not sponsors. Each serves a different role:

- **Production vendors** make content with us, usually for a fee or negotiated production arrangement.
- **Development companies** assess, develop, package, produce or acquire programming.
- **Broadcasters and networks** commission or distribute programming, generally through approved producers, representation or formal submission routes.
- **Creator platforms** may distribute completed or developing content under a revenue-share or licensing agreement.
- **FAST-channel infrastructure providers** can help build and operate an owned streaming channel.
- **Device/platform distributors** provide access to viewers; they do not normally fund or produce the show.
- **Public-sector and education partners** may provide locations, talent, training or conditional support, not guaranteed commercial sponsorship.

## 2. Development and creator platforms

### Studio Center Entertainment
- **Role:** Production/development company that publicly invites pitches for reality and original programming.
- **Potential value:** A possible development conversation if the concept fits its slate and submission terms.
- **DHF value:** Access to experienced development and production capabilities and a potential route toward packaging a show.
- **Risk:** A pitch submission can expose original format elements. The public page says submissions are not returned; it does not establish confidentiality, an NDA, payment terms or a rights-protective agreement.
- **Next step:** Review the exact submission terms, recipient, rights language and permitted use. Begin with a non-confidential overview only. Do not submit the format, episode engine, characters, scripts, budget or sizzle until approved.

Official route: https://studiocenter.com/content/sc-entertainment

### BVM+ / BlkVision Media Plus
- **Role:** Creator submission and distribution platform with public materials describing sponsor/revenue-share opportunities.
- **Potential value:** Distribution, monetization, audience development and a possible sponsor-sharing model for finished or appropriately packaged content.
- **DHF value:** A route to build audience and commercial history while retaining negotiating leverage for the underlying franchise.
- **Risk:** The current creator-submission page states that BVM+ offers a $500–$2,500 per-episode production stipend, 30%–50% of net sponsor revenue for creator-supplied sponsors, and exclusive streaming/promotional rights for two years while creators retain authorship credit. The page also describes a 1–2 episode/month suggested schedule, a minimum of 3 of 5 evaluation criteria, and an initial 2–4 week review. These are platform-published claims, not a negotiated offer. Authorship credit is not the same as ownership of the underlying IP. Verify the exact rights license, exclusivity, revenue deductions, sponsor approvals, term, territory, renewal, takedown and reversion in the actual contract.
- **Next step:** Ask for the current creator agreement and revenue model before submitting anything. Use a standalone, non-confidential creator project first; do not submit unreleased franchise IP.

Official route: https://www.blkvision.com/creator-submissions

### Blue Ant Media
- **Role:** Independent production, media and channel group with a formal pitch process.
- **Potential value:** Development, co-production, production services, channel commissioning or acquisition of completed content.
- **DHF value:** A possible path to a larger production partner and international distribution.
- **Risk:** Its submission process may request the hook, episode engine, hosts/characters, production team, budget and demo. Those items can disclose valuable format and franchise information.
- **Next step:** Confirm the exact channel/brand fit and submission rules. Build a separate, legally reviewed pitch package. Do not use the public media-partner inquiry form to submit the show.

Official route: https://blueantmedia.com/global-media/how-to-pitch-us/

### Palm TV Network
- **Role:** Streaming/creator platform with public submission guidelines.
- **Potential value:** A lower-barrier distribution conversation for completed or developing creator content and possible advertising/brand opportunities.
- **DHF value:** Additional distribution and audience testing before approaching larger networks.
- **Risk:** Verify that the platform is operating at the promised scale, actual distribution footprint, audience metrics, content moderation, rights license, revenue split, exclusivity and takedown terms. A submission guideline is not a guarantee of distribution or income.
- **Next step:** Verify current platform status and agreement terms; consider only non-confidential completed content first.

Official route: https://www.palmtvnetwork.com/submit-guidelines

## 3. Major network and broadcaster targets

- **Potential value:** National commissioning, licensing or distribution if a suitable producer packages the show.
- **DHF value:** Reach, production scale and national audience.
- **Boundary:** TLC and Food Network are not local sponsors and should not be treated as open direct-to-creator submission portals. Use an approved production company, agent/representative or verified submission route. Do not send unsolicited full concepts.

### NBCUniversal — Bravo / Peacock / NBC
- **Role:** Major broadcast, cable and streaming group.
- **Potential value:** Commissioning, licensing, streaming distribution or a future platform relationship.
- **DHF value:** Large-scale audience reach and a potential home for an established, packaged series.
- **Boundary:** These are long-term network targets, not first-dollar sponsors. A credible production package and accepted route are likely required. Keep the initial inquiry non-confidential.

Official company information: https://www.nbcuniversal.com/

## 4. Owned-channel and streaming distribution infrastructure

### acTVe
- **Role:** FAST-channel creation, channel operations, distribution/carriage and advertising/monetization infrastructure.
- **Potential value:** A route to build a branded Da HoodFarmer streaming channel with a controlled programming schedule and a commercial advertising model.
- **DHF value:** A potential way to move from pitching individual shows to operating an independent media destination.
- **Boundary:** This is infrastructure/distribution, not a show buyer or guaranteed sponsor. Verify setup fees, monthly operating costs, revenue share, ad inventory control, channel ownership, content rights, data access, platform carriage and exit terms.
- **Strategic importance:** High for the long-term independent-network objective, but not an immediate purchase. First establish a library of cleared content, audience demand, and a viable operating budget.

Official route: https://www.actve.tv/

### Roku
- **Role:** Streaming device/platform and app distribution ecosystem.
- **Potential value:** A future distribution endpoint for an owned DHF channel or app.
- **DHF value:** Access to Roku households and an established streaming platform.
- **Boundary:** Roku is not a production studio or sponsor. A distribution-ready app/channel, content rights, technical compliance and platform approval are required. Roku's developer documentation says the app should be published from the account of the organization that owns the content; if a third-party developer publishes under its own account, ownership of the app belongs to that developer under Roku's distribution agreement. DHF must retain the root developer account and grant vendors limited roles.

Developer information: https://developer.roku.com/

### Amazon Fire TV
- **Role:** Streaming device and app distribution ecosystem.
- **Potential value:** A second distribution endpoint for an owned DHF channel or app.
- **DHF value:** Additional reach and access to viewers using Fire TV devices.
- **Boundary:** Not a show producer or sponsor. Requires an app, cleared content rights, technical compliance and platform approval.

Developer information: https://developer.amazon.com/fire-tv

## 5. Public-sector, education and production-support resources

### South Carolina Film Commission
- **Role:** Production information, location support and state film/TV incentive administration.
- **Potential value:** Location guidance, production referrals and qualifying incentive support.
- **Important limitation:** The cash rebate requires at least $1 million in qualifying South Carolina spend, pre-approval and an eligible production. The official FAQ explicitly says unscripted/reality TV series are not eligible for the cash rebate. The FAQ separately describes a possible discretionary sales/use/accommodations tax exemption for certain projects spending at least $250,000; that is a tax exemption, not a cash rebate. Do not include a rebate or exemption in a budget without written eligibility confirmation.
- **DHF value:** Location and production planning support; not a sponsor or guaranteed grant.

Official information: https://www.scprt.com/film-commission/incentives

### South Carolina Arts Commission
- **Role:** Arts grants and public arts support.
- **Potential value:** Possible project support if the applicant, project and current grant program qualify.
- **Important limitation:** Eligibility can depend on applicant type, nonprofit/fiscal-sponsor status, discipline, matching funds and grant cycle. A for-profit media company should not assume eligibility.
- **DHF value:** A possible funding path for a qualifying public arts/education project, not guaranteed operating capital or sponsorship.

Official grants: https://www.southcarolinaarts.com/all-grants/

### University of South Carolina — School of Visual Art and Design
- **Role:** Education, student talent and creative collaboration.
- **Potential value:** Student projects, internships, crew/talent introductions, educational collaboration or production research.
- **DHF value:** A potential talent-development and local collaboration pathway.
- **Boundary:** Not a sponsor or network. Any student participation, university name, location or logo requires the university's own approval and appropriate releases.

## 6. Recommended partnership ladder

1. **Build the public content library locally.** Qualify Carolina Film Studio, As Told By and OneSixEight for clearly scoped public-facing production. Keep costs within the existing approval limit.
2. **Grow distribution with finished, non-confidential work.** Evaluate BVM+ and Palm TV after verifying current terms, revenue splits and rights.
3. **Prepare a legally reviewed development package.** Only after the show format and ownership protections are documented should Studio Center or Blue Ant receive a formal pitch.
4. **Approach major networks through the right route.** TLC/Food Network and Bravo/Peacock/NBC are long-term targets; use an accepted production-company or representation route.
5. **Build the owned channel when content and audience justify it.** Evaluate acTVe for FAST infrastructure and Roku/Fire TV for distribution. Do not pay for channel infrastructure before a content library, budget and audience plan exist.
6. **Use public-sector and education resources only where eligible.** Confirm Film Commission classification and Arts Commission applicant rules before building any funding assumptions.

## 7. DHF intellectual-property and deal guardrails

The long-term objective is to retain ownership and control of the Da HoodFarmer brand, original show formats, underlying concepts, characters, fictional worlds, AI character identities, scripts, production assets, merchandise and future derivative opportunities.

Working negotiating targets from the owner's prior direction:
- Prefer 100% underlying IP ownership.
- Consider 75%+ ownership/control only with clearly limited, valuable partner rights.
- Do not accept less than 51% ownership/control without a separate, explicit owner decision and legal review.

These are negotiating objectives, not legal guarantees. Each contract must define:
- The exact project and materials licensed.
- Media, territory, term and exclusivity.
- Whether the partner receives an option, license, production right or ownership interest.
- Rights to sequels, spin-offs, remakes, merchandising, games, live events, international versions and AI-generated derivatives.
- Whether the partner may use materials for AI training or model improvement (default: no).
- Approval rights, credits, compensation, revenue accounting, audit rights and deductions.
- Reversion/termination if the project is not produced or distributed.
- Restrictions on sublicensing, assignment and use of the brand or characters.

Do not assume an NDA will be offered. Do not send a full concept merely because a submission form exists. Keep dated copies of every version and a record of what was disclosed, to whom and under which terms.

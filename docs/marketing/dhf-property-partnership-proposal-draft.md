# DHF Land & Experiences — Property Partnership Proposal

**Internal draft — November 2026**  
**Purpose:** Establish a low-capital partnership model for farms, ranches, rural venues, landowners and tourism operators.

## Partnership concept

DHF identifies, packages and markets suitable experiences in collaboration with property owners. The owner retains control of the property. DHF may contribute the concept, audience development, event organization, vendor coordination, content and customer acquisition under a written agreement.

No property is listed, advertised as available, accessed or used until the owner authorizes the specific activity and the parties sign appropriate terms.

## Potential programs

- Agricultural tours and field days.
- Specialty crop and harvest experiences.
- Livestock and equine demonstrations.
- Outdoor education and supervised survival-skills workshops.
- Vendor markets and agricultural festivals.
- Photography, filming and content-production location bookings.
- Private group experiences and corporate retreats.
- Farm-to-table and seasonal experiences, subject to food-service requirements.

## Partnership models

1. **Fixed site rental:** DHF pays an agreed fee for a defined time and permitted use.
2. **Revenue share:** The owner receives an agreed share of gross or net event revenue, with expenses defined in writing.
3. **Minimum guarantee plus share:** A minimum fee plus a share above an agreed revenue threshold.
4. **Co-produced event:** Both parties contribute defined resources and share revenue and responsibilities.
5. **Location license:** A production or photography company pays for a defined use window and location rights.

No model is selected until the property, proposed activity, costs, responsibilities and insurance requirements are reviewed.

## DHF responsibilities — proposed

- Develop the event or experience concept.
- Prepare a marketing and customer-acquisition plan.
- Manage applications, registration and customer communications.
- Coordinate vendors, speakers or instructors as agreed.
- Provide agreed event-day operations and post-event reporting.
- Collect and reconcile payments only through an approved payment process.

## Property partner responsibilities — to be negotiated

- Confirm legal authority to offer the site.
- Disclose known site restrictions and access conditions.
- Identify permitted areas, capacity and operating hours.
- Confirm utilities, sanitation, parking, accessibility and emergency access.
- Provide required permits, licenses or insurance where assigned.
- Approve all public descriptions, images and uses of the property.

## Required due diligence before any event

- Ownership or authorized management verification.
- Zoning and land-use permission for the proposed activity.
- Event permits and local approvals.
- General liability and activity-specific insurance.
- Animal welfare and handling requirements where applicable.
- Food handling, sanitation and water requirements where applicable.
- Fire, weather, emergency response and participant safety plans.
- Accessibility, parking, traffic and capacity.
- Written cancellation, weather, damage, cleanup and indemnity terms.

## Commercial worksheet

Before pricing, estimate:

- Maximum safe attendance and usable event area.
- Site fee or revenue-share basis.
- Insurance, permits, sanitation, security and cleanup.
- Staff, instructors, equipment, utilities and vendor needs.
- Ticket price and expected conversion.
- Refund, cancellation and weather contingency.
- Owner payment timing and DHF delivery costs.

A property partnership should proceed only when the expected economics support both parties and the operating plan is safe and lawful.

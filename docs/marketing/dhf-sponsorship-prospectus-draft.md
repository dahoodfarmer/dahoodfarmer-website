# DHF Opportunity Network — Sponsorship Prospectus

**Draft for prospective partner review — November 2026**  
**Not a binding offer. Event dates, audience figures, inventory and final pricing will be confirmed in a written agreement.**

## Partner with a growing platform

Da HoodFarmer is developing a national business, agriculture, media, education and event network. The DHF Opportunity Network creates opportunities for businesses and institutions to connect with entrepreneurs, agricultural producers, creators, vendors, landowners and community organizations.

We welcome prospective partners from all 50 U.S. states and consider international partnerships where the program, applicable requirements and logistics align.

## Sponsorship opportunities

| Package | Proposed pilot investment | Availability | Core benefits |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Community Partner | $250 | Limited | Sponsor recognition on the event page and one social recognition |
| Supporting Sponsor | $500 | Up to 5 | Logo placement, one newsletter mention and one social mention |
| Founding Sponsor | $1,000 | Up to 3 | Supporting benefits plus sponsor interview or short presentation and two admissions |
| Presenting Partner | $2,500 | One per event | “Presented by” placement, opening/closing recognition, premium digital exhibit, sponsor interview, up to three social mentions and four admissions |
| In-kind Partner | Negotiated | By fit | Defined contribution and agreed recognition/deliverables |

All amounts are proposed pilot rates, not yet approved for public sale. The final offer may vary by event format, geography, audience, deliverables and costs.

## Potential sponsor alignment

- Agriculture, equipment, seed, food systems and rural services.
- Small business, finance, professional services and entrepreneurship.
- Technology, AI, media, education and workforce development.
- Tourism, land stewardship, outdoor recreation and hospitality.
- Consumer brands aligned with the event's audience and purpose.

## What sponsors receive

Every package will identify the event or program, placement, content, timing, admission or exhibit inventory, reporting and payment terms. Paid interviews, promotions and other sponsored content will be clearly disclosed.

We do not guarantee attendance, impressions, leads, sales or return on investment unless a specific written agreement expressly defines a measurable deliverable.

## First proposed flagship

**DHF Agricultural Innovation & Entrepreneurship Summit — virtual pilot**

A proposed program connecting agricultural producers, entrepreneurs, educators, service providers and technology innovators. The virtual format is intended to validate demand before committing to a physical venue.

No date, speaker, venue, audience size or sponsor is confirmed at this stage.

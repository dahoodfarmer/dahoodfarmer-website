# DHF Opportunity Network — Internal Sales Kit & Founding Rate Card

**Status:** Internal draft for owner review. Not published. Prices are proposed pilot rates, not active checkout products or binding offers.  
**Market:** All 50 U.S. states; international inquiries accepted where legal, payment, tax, insurance and logistics requirements can be met.

We own the customer relationship, program concepts, brand, event format, media and application pipeline. We rent or partner for facilities, equipment and property when needed. Applications are free; payment is requested only after a specific offer is approved and documented.

## Customer acquisition funnel

1. Organic content, partnerships, directory discovery and referrals.
2. Free application or event-interest registration.
3. Review and qualification by DHF.
4. Written offer with exact deliverables, price, timing and cancellation terms.
5. Secure payment through the existing DHF payment engine after the payment product is activated.
6. Delivery and evidence of fulfillment.
7. Renewal, repeat event, upsell or sponsor renewal.

## Payment integration and controls

# Da HoodFarmer — Sponsor Overview

**Internal draft for owner review | November 2026**
**Not a binding offer. No audience statistics or performance guarantees are represented in this document.**

## A platform for agriculture, business, education, media, and experiences

Da HoodFarmer is building an independent platform that connects practical agriculture, entrepreneurship, rural and western lifestyle, education, creator media, and business opportunity.

We are developing sponsor opportunities for companies that want a clearly scoped way to support relevant programs and reach audiences through useful content, educational resources, business features, and future events.

## Five partnership areas

1. **Business Network** — business profiles, entrepreneur resources, and sponsored business features.
2. **Summits & Events** — defined sponsor recognition, sessions, and event-specific integrations.
3. **Marketplace** — clearly labeled brand and vendor showcases, product demonstrations, and supplier resources.
4. **Academy** — sponsor-supported educational resources and workshops.
5. **Land & Experiences** — approved field days, demonstrations, outdoor education, and experience-based programs.

## Working sponsorship levels

| Partnership | Working investment | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Community Partner | $250/month | Sponsor directory recognition and one agreed monthly recognition placement |
| Featured Partner | $500/month | Featured placement, organization profile, and one agreed monthly promotional feature |
| Corporate Partner | $1,000/month | Premium placement across an agreed division, brand integration, and one agreed content or program opportunity |
| Presenting Partner | $2,500+ per campaign/event | Presenting recognition and negotiated premium event/campaign benefits |

These are working pilot price anchors, not active checkout products or binding offers. Final availability, placements, timing, and deliverables are confirmed in a written agreement.

## Sponsor safeguards

- Paid placements and sponsored content are clearly labeled.
- Editorial decisions remain independent; sponsors cannot purchase favorable coverage or guaranteed conclusions.
- No unverified audience, traffic, attendance, impression, lead, sales, or ROI claims.
- No category exclusivity unless expressly negotiated in writing.
- No sponsor logo or endorsement claim is published before written approval.
- Sponsor payment is requested only after an approved written scope and an authorized payment workflow exist.

## What we need from a prospective partner

Tell us your brand objectives, target geography, relevant audience, preferred program area, timing, and approximate budget range. DHF will assess fit and prepare a specific proposal with defined deliverables.

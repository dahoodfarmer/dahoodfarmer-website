## Public-facing identity

Da HoodFarmer is an independent agriculture, western-lifestyle, entrepreneurship, education, and creator-media brand based in South Carolina.

The brand's public-facing subject areas include:
- Practical agriculture, livestock, land stewardship, and rural enterprise.
- Western lifestyle, outdoor skills, and hands-on learning.
- Entrepreneurship, independent business, and community opportunity.
- Creator storytelling, interviews, educational video, and digital media.

## Potential media relationships

We are open to exploring appropriately scoped conversations with:
- Television and streaming production companies.
- Independent producers and development teams.
- Podcast studios and audio networks.
- Digital publishers and creator-media platforms.
- Regional broadcasters and documentary teams.
- Brand-content and production partners.

Potential collaboration formats may include interviews, guest appearances, sponsored segments, digital series, podcast conversations, production services, co-produced educational content, and distribution discussions. No project, format, schedule, or rights package is represented as commissioned or confirmed.

## Collaboration principles

- Keep an initial conversation high-level and non-confidential.
- Review each recipient's submission policy before sending materials.
- Do not send scripts, formats, episode outlines, unreleased characters, budgets, sizzle materials, casting plans, or production mechanics through an unsolicited inquiry.
- Do not assume a network, studio, or producer will sign an NDA.
- Keep dated copies of original materials and records of what was shared.
- Clarify rights, ownership, credits, compensation, exclusivity, options, and permitted use before any development or production commitment.
- Obtain qualified legal review of submission terms and agreements when confidential materials or rights are involved.
